# Product audit deep pass 1.6

Research checked 22 September 2026. This pass adds 24 exact product or range records across Waitrose, M&S, Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Morrisons, Lidl, Aldi and Asda.

The audit separates:

- organic product claims and control codes;
- free-range or outdoor claims;
- welfare schemes such as RSPCA Assured, Better Chicken Commitment and Red Tractor Enhanced Welfare;
- range-level policy evidence; and
- exact product-level evidence.

Welfare-only claims do not enter Fieldfare’s organic evidence filter. They remain visible as pack checks, comparisons or unknown evidence. Range pages are marked for a live pack check where they do not expose a current SKU, control code or certificate scope.

The detailed source dataset is retained in `work/deep-product-audit.json` and the research notes in `work/deep-product-audit.md`.
