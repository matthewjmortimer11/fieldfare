# Retailer data-feed request drafts

These drafts request permission and data. They do not authorise site crawling. Add the sender details, the live-app link and [CATALOGUE-IMPORT-SPEC.md](CATALOGUE-IMPORT-SPEC.md) before sending.

## Shared request

**Subject:** Request for licensed meat-catalogue data for Fieldfare

Hello [retailer team],

I’m building Fieldfare, an independent consumer app that helps people reduce their reliance on factory-farmed meat by finding products with clearly documented farming and certification evidence. We would like written permission and a machine-readable feed covering meat, poultry, charcuterie and meat-containing prepared products sold online and, where possible, in Scottish stores.

For each currently ranged sellable variant, we need retailer product ID, GTIN/EAN where available, name, brand/range, pack size, species/category, ingredients, origin, current product URL, image URL, availability/assortment context and an updated timestamp. We will display retailer attribution and source links, refresh or remove withdrawn products, and keep unreviewed products visibly unreviewed. We will not infer animal-welfare standards from absent data, price, country of origin, premium branding or a barcode.

Could you confirm the correct contact and whether a data licence, feed, partnership route or manual export is available? We would also welcome definitions and supporting evidence for your organic, free-range, outdoor-bred, RSPCA Assured, Better Chicken Commitment, Red Tractor and equivalent range fields.

Kind regards,

[name]  
[role / Fieldfare]  
[website]  
[email]
