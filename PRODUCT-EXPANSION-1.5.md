# Product expansion 1.5

Research checked 22 September 2026. This pass adds 18 mainstream supermarket records across Aldi, Co-op and Lidl. Product identity and welfare wording are recorded separately from organic approval; none of these additions qualifies as organic without an exact organic claim.

The full source notes and URLs are retained in `work/product-round2.md` and the import source in `work/product-round2.json`:

- Aldi: lamb mince, turkey mince, free-range thighs, turkey steaks, mixed mince, seasonal turkey, pork medallions and lamb chops.
- Co-op: chicken breast, Scottish rump, outdoor-bred pork loin, outdoor-bred sausage, turkey mince and British meatballs.
- Lidl: grass-fed sirloin, lamb joint, outdoor-bred sausages and Deluxe turkey.

Range and policy pages are marked for pack checking where they do not identify the exact current SKU or control scope. Missing evidence is never treated as proof of poor welfare.
