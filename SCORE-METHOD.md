# Evidence scoring v1 — 22 September 2026

The 100-point score describes documented support for the organic shopping standard. It is an editorial evidence-coverage rubric, not a validated welfare assessment, probability, certification or numerical measure of suffering. The buying verdict is computed separately. No score threshold approves a product.

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Explicit organic claim for exact product | 25 |
| Product control code observed | 25 (18 if only certifier named) |
| Rearing-system evidence recorded | 15 |
| Linked primary-source material | 10 |
| Reviewed within 90 days and no unresolved pack conflict | 10 |
| Current certificate/product scope independently checked | 10 |
| Pack GTIN independently matched | 5 |

Known indoor rearing earns evidence points but fails an outdoor requirement. Unknown facts earn no points, which does not establish poor welfare. Current records earn no independent certificate/GTIN points. Older records lose freshness points. The interface exposes every dimension and limitation.

Retailer coverage is the number meeting the selected standard divided by all reviewed products for that retailer. Its denominator and selected-sample limitation are always shown. It is not a chain-wide welfare ranking or a representative availability estimate. Strict certificate preferences change the percentage; product evidence points remain independent of preferences.

Tests cover exact rubric examples, bounds, stale evidence, no score override, zero-sample coverage, strict preferences and offline brand assets.
